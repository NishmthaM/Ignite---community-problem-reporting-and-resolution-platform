We’re here to help

Help & contact

Find answers or contact the municipal team.

What happens after I submit a report?

You receive a reference number. Municipal staff review the details, check for related reports, and publish status updates as work progresses.

How are duplicate reports handled?

Staff may link reports about the same issue. Each submission keeps its own reference number, and the main report is used to share progress.

Why is my report still under review?

Staff may need to verify the issue, determine responsibility, or request more information before accepting and assigning work.

Can I report without sharing my location?

Yes. Location is optional, though a street or nearby landmark can help staff find the issue.

When will the issue be fixed?

Timing depends on urgency, available resources, and the work required. Follow your report for recorded updates.

Is this an emergency service?

No. This website is for non-emergency community issues. For immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency service.
